Archymedes is a small startup building defensive and civil simulation prototypes from public data (battery thermal runaway,
soft-armor screening, sensor-fault diagnosis, aerodynamics with measured error bars). Owner asks for ONE synthetic 3D world that
approaches reality, rebuilt from open data (terrain, imagery, buildings, roads), where drones, robot dogs, ground vehicles and
sensors run patrol, search-and-rescue and convoy missions in the same persistent world. Usefulness and defensibility first,
heavy invariant testing. Scope rule: no weapon effects, targeting or engagement; no military or sensitive real sites.
Facts:
- Dev machine: 4 CPU cores, 7 GB RAM, no GPU. Isaac Sim / Unreal / Omniverse cannot run locally; rented GPU possible later.
- Existing web app: Next.js 16, React 19, three.js via react-three-fiber, Tailwind 4, password-gated.
- Swiss site (Lauterbrunnen, 4x4 km): swisstopo open data: swissALTI3D 0.5 m DTM, SWISSIMAGE 10 cm, swissSURFACE3D classified
  lidar, swissBUILDINGS3D 3.0 LoD2 roofs for every building (usable as hidden ground truth). About 1 GB without lidar.
- US site (Boulder foothills, 4x4 km): USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM, DRCOG 2020 lidar (~250 MB per km2 tile), NAIP 0.6 m imagery,
  Overture/OSM buildings; no open LoD2, so lidar class-6 points are the building truth.
- Kigali: Copernicus 30 m DEM, Sentinel-2 10 m, Google Open Buildings 2.5D; poor data, strongest test of deduction.
- Netherlands: AHN lidar + 3DBAG LoD2 nationwide, very rich but flat.
- Market: Cesium/Bentley, Bohemia VBS4, Duality Falcon, NVIDIA Omniverse/Isaac, AirSim (archived), CARLA, Parallel Domain,
  Applied Intuition sell or ship synthetic environments; none ships per-feature provenance and measured realism error bands.
- Open autonomy stacks: PX4/ArduPilot SITL, ROS 2 Nav2, MuJoCo Menagerie (Unitree Go2, ANYmal) run on CPU.
